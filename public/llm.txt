# Agritex Global (7 Horse's Export)

> Precision sourcing for sustainable scale — export of premium Indian agro commodities and handloom textiles to global markets.

## Overview

Agritex Global (trading as 7 Horse's Export) is an Indian export company specializing in two verticals:

- **Agro** — Fresh produce and staples sourced directly from Indian farming regions (onions, lemons, chillies, mangoes, bananas, ginger, capsicum, basmati rice)
- **Textile** — Handloom and artisan textiles including silk sarees, block-printed cotton, dyed sarees, and combed cotton fabrics
